1. What tokens are

Tokens are how you pay for AI Foundation's products and services. You exchange money for a token. Tokens mean more flexible payment options, as a payment may be distributed to multiple service providers. In MY ASX Options, for example, tokens unlock a contract's value, scope and sources.

The default price for one token is AUD $1. We never use decimals; this business only uses whole numbers and we round up all numbers to make them whole numbers.

The number of tokens something costs is always shown before you spend them.

2. Buying tokens

You buy tokens at the price shown at checkout.

Example: you buy 100 tokens for US$100. Those 100 tokens are added to your token account, and you can use them for our products and services.

We may change the price of tokens. A change applies from when it's shown, and doesn't affect tokens you already have.

3. Australian dollars at checkout

We're an Australian business, so when you check out, the price of your tokens is converted to Australian dollars and you're charged in Australian dollars. All invoices and transactions are issued strictly in Australian Dollars (AUD).

If your card or bank account is in another currency, your bank or card provider converts the charge, and may add its own fees. Any currency conversion to USD shown in the app is an indicative estimate only.

4. Sharing tokens

You can share tokens with other users, such as friends and family. Each share costs 1 token, on top of the tokens you share.

Example: you share 20 tokens with a family member. They receive 20 tokens, and 21 tokens come out of your token account: the 20 you shared and 1 for the share.

5. Paying out tokens

If your token account holds more than 100 tokens, you can ask us to pay it out. We need 72 hours' notice.

  • To ask, email support@aifoundation.net.au from the email address on your account, and tell us the bank account to pay.
  • We pay out in Australian dollars, and confirm the amount with you before we pay.
  • If your bank account is outside Australia, the payment should reach your account in your local currency, converted by the banks involved.

6. International fees

You're responsible for all international wire transfer fees and surcharges, whether they're charged by your bank, our bank or any bank in between. They may reduce the amount that reaches your account.

7. Your rights & refunds

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or changes any right you have under the Australian Consumer Law. Our Terms of Service also cover tokens.

Refund rules: Refund requests are to be made in writing by sending a message to the superadmin at support@aifoundation.net.au. Messages to superadmin are always free.
