2. MY ASX Options in brief

MY ASX Options is a daily brief of Australian government contracts. It lists contracts confirmed in the past 7 days and decisions expected in the next 7, links the companies involved to their ASX codes where it can, shows whether those companies have exchange-traded options, and sums up the key takeaways.
