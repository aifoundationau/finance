      A note from our founder
      
          
      Welcome to MY ASX Options
      
          
      Australian governments sign contracts worth billions of dollars every week. When an ASX-listed company wins one, it can move the share price, but the news is spread across AusTender, ASX announcements and dozens of news sites.
      
          
      MY ASX Options brings it together in one brief each day: contracts confirmed in the past 7 days, decisions expected in the next 7, the ASX code of each company we can match, whether it has exchange-traded options, and the key takeaways.
      
          
      
            
      “We built MY ASX Options so you can see government contract news in one place, check it against the source, and make up your own mind.”
      
            
      Founder, AI Foundation
