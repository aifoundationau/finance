Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

MY ASX Options is a daily brief of Australian government contracts, linked to ASX-listed companies and their exchange-traded options. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who can see it, how we protect it and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

At a glance:

  • You can read the brief without signing in.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. You become a member of AI Foundation, with one account across AI Foundation's apps.
  • The brief is built from public sources about companies and governments. Our AI doesn't receive any information about you.
  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time: email support@aifoundation.net.au.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to MY ASX Options ("we", "us" and "our"), the website at finance.aifoundation.net.au, and the services we provide through it. Please read it together with our Terms of Service and Token Policy.

"Personal information" means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, as defined in the Privacy Act.

2. Who we are

MY ASX Options is run by AI Foundation, based in Australia. You can contact us about privacy at support@aifoundation.net.au.

3. The information we collect

When you sign in

You register and sign in with your Google account. Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID with us. We never receive your Google password.

Your account is an AI Foundation account, shared by AI Foundation's apps. When you first sign in, we create it (or find the one you already have) and record your name, email address, profile picture, your role (everyone starts as a Supporter), when you registered and signed in last, and that you registered through MY ASX Options.

Tokens

When tokens are in use, we keep a token account for you: your balance, the contracts you've unlocked, and a record of the tokens added, shared and spent. If you ask us to pay out tokens, we collect the bank account details we need to pay you (see our Token Policy).

Online payments aren't connected to MY ASX Options yet. When they are, the payment provider will collect your card details directly, and we'll name it in this policy first.

Your country

We show prices in the currencies for your country. To work out your country, we use the country your internet connection comes from (which our hosting provider works out from your IP address), and your browser's time zone and language. We use this for each visit and don't store it.

Device and usage information

Our servers and hosting provider record technical information when you use MY ASX Options, such as your IP address, browser, the pages and features you use, the date and time, and any errors. Our own records of sign-ins include your account ID and role. We use this to run, secure and fix MY ASX Options.

If you contact us

If you email us, we keep your message, your contact details and our reply.

4. How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage your account and role;
  • keep your token account, unlock contract details you've paid for, and pay out tokens when you ask;
  • show prices in the right currencies;
  • provide support, and answer questions and complaints;
  • keep MY ASX Options secure, prevent misuse and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • fix problems and improve MY ASX Options; and
  • meet our legal obligations, such as tax and record-keeping laws.

We use personal information only for the purpose we collected it for, for a related purpose you would reasonably expect, or as the law otherwise allows.

5. AI

When our administrator switches it on, the AI analyst uses Anthropic's Claude, with web search, to research public news and announcements and write the daily brief. It works only with information about companies, contracts and government agencies. It never receives your account details or anything else about you, and we don't use AI to make decisions about you.

Our AI Policy explains how we manage AI, following ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

6. Who can see your information

  • AI Foundation's administrators can see your AI Foundation account (name, email address, picture and role), which AI Foundation's apps share.
  • MY ASX Options' administrator can see token accounts and their records, to add tokens, check balances and answer questions.
  • Other users can't see your account or your token account.

We share personal information outside AI Foundation only:

  • with the service providers that help us run MY ASX Options (see section 7);
  • when the law requires or allows it, for example to comply with a court order or a lawful request from a government agency;
  • to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, AI Foundation or others, including to investigate fraud or misuse;
  • with a buyer or successor if all or part of our business is sold or restructured, so they can keep providing the service under this policy; and
  • with your consent.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't give it to others for their own marketing.

7. Service providers and overseas disclosure

These providers handle personal information for us, for the services they provide:

  • Google: Google sign-in, and Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, where accounts and our data are stored;
  • Vercel: hosting the website and running our servers; and
  • Anthropic: the AI analyst. It receives research instructions about public news, not information about you.

Our servers also fetch public market and contract data, from sources such as AusTender, the ASX, Yahoo Finance, the Frankfurter exchange-rate service, Google News and news publishers. Your browser doesn't connect to them, and we don't send them information about you.

Some providers store or process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States, where our database is located. Before we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to make sure it will be handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, such as choosing reputable providers with strong security and privacy commitments.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS), and encryption at rest by our hosting providers.
  • Sign-in through Google, so we never hold your password.
  • Sign-in sessions in signed cookies that scripts in the page can't read.
  • A database that browsers can't reach directly: only our servers read and change it, after checking who's asking.
  • Administrator access only for listed, Google-verified email addresses.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your Google account secure, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your AI Foundation account: while it's open, and for a reasonable time after it closes, to resolve questions and disputes.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Token accounts, payout and transaction records: as long as tax, accounting and financial laws require, usually 5 to 7 years.
  • Server logs: for the limited time our hosting provider keeps them.

When we no longer need personal information, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Access: you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: you can ask us to correct information that's inaccurate, out of date, incomplete or misleading.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to close your account and delete your information. Your account is shared by AI Foundation's apps, so closing it closes it in all of them. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it (see section 9).
  • Signing out: sign out at any time from your account, at the top right of the page.
  • Cookies and browser storage: you can clear them in your browser (see section 11).

To make a request, email support@aifoundation.net.au from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first. We'll respond within 30 days, and we won't charge you for making a request.

11. Cookies and browser storage

MY ASX Options uses cookies and browser storage only to make the site work and remember your choices. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other websites.

  • Sign-in (mao_user): keeps you signed in. Kept for 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Google sign-in in progress (mao_oauth): links Google's reply to your sign-in, to protect it. Kept for 10 minutes.
  • Administrator session (mao_admin): only for our administrator. Kept for 12 hours.
  • Token account (mao_wallet): links this browser to its token account, when tokens are in use. Kept for 400 days.
  • Colour theme (browser storage, mao-theme): remembers light or dark mode. Kept until you clear it.

If you block or delete these, you'll need to sign in again, and some features may not work.

12. Children

MY ASX Options is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or the law changes. We'll publish the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and tell you in MY ASX Options or by email before a significant change takes effect.

14. Complaints

If you're concerned about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first at support@aifoundation.net.au. We'll look into it and respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.
